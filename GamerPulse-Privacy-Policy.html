Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Dark Doctor (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how GamerPulse accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the GamerPulse Windows application.

GamerPulse is a PC gaming library and save-game manager that helps users discover installed games, organize and launch their collection, view game information, and manage local save backups.

1. Information GamerPulse accesses

To provide its features, GamerPulse may access information available on your computer, including:

  • Installed game titles, installation paths, and supported launcher information from Steam, Epic Games, Xbox/Game Pass, and GOG.
  • Game-save files and local backup locations.
  • Game artwork, titles, metadata, and supported game activity or playtime information.
  • Application preferences, library settings, and backup configuration.

This information supports game discovery, organization, launching, display of game information, and save backups.

2. Local storage and save backups

GamerPulse is designed to store its core application data, library information, settings, and save-game backups locally on your computer. Your game library and save files are not intentionally uploaded to Dark Doctor servers.

Save backups are stored in locations configured by you or by the application. You are responsible for choosing suitable backup locations and maintaining additional copies you want to keep.

3. YouTube Data API and embedded videos

GamerPulse uses the YouTube Data API to search for publicly available videos related to games in your library, such as gameplay videos, trailers, reviews, and previews.

  • When you use video discovery, game titles or related search terms may be sent to the YouTube Data API.
  • GamerPulse may receive video details such as titles, thumbnails, channel information, and video identifiers.
  • Selected videos are presented through YouTube's official embedded player.
  • GamerPulse does not download, modify, or redistribute YouTube videos.

Google and YouTube may collect information through their APIs, services, and embedded player under their own policies.

4. Third-party gaming platforms

GamerPulse can work with locally installed games and launcher information associated with Steam, Epic Games, Xbox/Game Pass, and GOG. These platforms are independently operated and have their own privacy policies and terms. GamerPulse is not affiliated with or endorsed by these companies.

5. Information sharing

Dark Doctor does not sell personal information. GamerPulse does not intentionally send your local game library, game-save files, or backup contents to Dark Doctor. Information is exchanged with third-party services when needed for features you use, such as YouTube video searches and playback.

6. Data retention and deletion

Core GamerPulse library data, settings, caches, and save backups are generally stored locally on your device. You can remove local GamerPulse data using available in-app deletion controls and/or by deleting the relevant GamerPulse application-data and backup folders from your computer.

Uninstalling GamerPulse may not automatically remove all application data or save backups. Review your local folders before deleting them, and keep any backups you wish to retain.

GamerPulse does not maintain a Dark Doctor account containing your game library or save files, and Dark Doctor does not have a server-side copy of those local files to delete. YouTube-related requests are handled by Google; information held by Google is subject to Google's policies and privacy controls.

7. Security

GamerPulse uses local storage for its core functionality. No software or storage method can guarantee complete security. Protect your computer and maintain independent copies of important files.

8. Children's privacy

GamerPulse is a general-purpose PC gaming utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the application.

9. Your choices

You may choose not to use optional YouTube video discovery features, stop using GamerPulse, or uninstall the application. You can manage local application data and backups on your device.

10. Policy updates

We may update this policy when GamerPulse's features or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when it was most recently revised.
